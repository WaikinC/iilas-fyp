## What we could develop ourselves

The missing work is a collection, labelling and evaluation process; new AI architectures are unnecessary. Existing intake templates and validators in [DATA_COLLECTION.md](DATA_COLLECTION.md) provide a starting point. The user's current public-only collection choice remains unchanged by this research answer.

1. Inventory: collect dated product snapshots with actual reservations, reorder policy versions and preceding movement/adjustment/receipt events. Keep separate evidence of commitments, inbound supply and fulfilment risk. Independent reviewers apply the three report classes with disagreements retained. Define available-stock semantics before deriving fields.
2. Delivery: record pseudonymous shipment/parcel links, dispatch and deadline revisions, each event's occurrence and recorded-at time, failed attempts, proof capture/upload/verification, and completed/returned outcomes. Build inputs only from information actually available at the agreed snapshot. Use later outcomes only for a separately agreed outcome-based target; incomplete follow-up stays unknown. If the target is contemporaneous expert risk, label it as such and assess reviewer agreement.
3. Parcel: start a capture-procedure pilot using owned/reused empty boxes. Track physical box, session, device, visible condition and natural versus staged damage. Label the visible condition of each image; obscured damage on another side does not automatically label this view Damaged. Keep every view, before/after state, crop and augmentation of one box in the same partition. Collect both classes across comparable backgrounds and devices.
4. Simulation: an explicit inventory option under Wong's report. Build coherent histories rather than independent random rows; preserve conservation of stock and reservation/receipt timing. A model trained to reproduce a rule is evaluated as rule imitation. Simulated or staged results cannot establish real deployment accuracy. A delivery simulation would need its own accepted scope and protocol.
5. Evaluation: freeze group-separated and appropriate temporal partitions before tuning; fit preprocessing on training data only. Reserve independent later real cases for operational claims. Report synthetic, staged-real and natural operational results separately, including class precision/recall, macro F1, confusion counts and uncertainty. See [scikit-learn leakage guidance](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/common_pitfalls.html).

## Recommended next decision

First audit the small hospital, URBANE and Peru files for actual column definitions, timestamps, grouping and terms. Review Kaputt access terms and metadata before any bounded image acquisition. Retain the existing benchmarks and original confirmation reservation. If the new sources still lack primary labels and histories, choose explicitly between a disclosed public/simulated prototype evaluation and an authorised primary-data collection pilot.

Do not join unrelated datasets row-by-row to manufacture missing reservations, attempts or proof. More records do not establish better accuracy. No additional application feature, new training, model activation, production operation, author contact or publication was performed in this review.
