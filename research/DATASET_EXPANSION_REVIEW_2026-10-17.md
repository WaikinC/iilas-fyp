# Public dataset expansion review — 17 October 2026

The user requested further web research where the current data are insufficient, aiming to improve precision and accuracy. This review used the installed Parallel Search plugin, official publisher/repository pages and original papers, with separate inventory and delivery research agents. It adds source recommendations and intake checks only: no dataset was downloaded, relabelled, merged or trained, and no existing evaluation was repeated. Additional features remain deferred.

## What needs more data

| Module | Current local evidence | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Parcel condition | 40 synthetic images; 34 review candidates and six quarantined; 27 precautionary groups preserving 30 asset roots; no human-approved labels | Too small and unresolved for a defensible final MobileNetV2/EfficientNetB0 comparison. Prioritise more independent parcel families and reviewed images, with separate real-image evidence. |
| Inventory backorder benchmark | 1,929,935 records; rare positive class; RF test precision 6.76%, recall 80.98% | This is already a large dataset. Poor precision alone does not establish insufficient sample size. Imbalance, prediction-time feature quality, upstream transformations, population mismatch and decision threshold need investigation. |
| Delivery lateness benchmark | 172,765 item records across 62,897 orders; RF test precision 89.54%, recall 55.42%, accuracy 70.59% | The immediate weakness is missed late cases. Precision is not accuracy. More rows without better pre-outcome information may leave the same limitation. |

The baseline values come from the existing local benchmark reports; they are historical observed test results. No additional source establishes that a higher score will result. Accuracy alone is misleading for rare backorders because an all-negative classifier can be highly accurate while finding no backorders. Learning curves can help test sample-size limitations, but adding those fits requires a recorded protocol extension before execution; this review does not alter the existing 34-fit plan.

## Recommended sources for intake audit

Counts below are publisher metadata, not locally verified download counts. A candidate is not yet an accepted training dataset.

| Priority / module | Source and verified scope | Intended use and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — Parcel | [Original Parcel3D release](https://zenodo.org/records/8032204): over 13,000 intact/damaged synthetic images; 45.8 GB full archive; academic/noncommercial use with mixed component restrictions | Expand the existing pilot from the same original release, prioritising new base families rather than near-duplicate renders. Retain configurations, source labels and family provenance. Synthetic performance cannot establish real-world accuracy. The publisher's full archive size is not a recommendation to download all 45.8 GB. |
| 2 — Inventory | [FreshRetailNet-50K, original Dingdong release](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Dingdong-Inc/FreshRetailNet-50K): 50,000 store-product series, 898 stores, 4.5 million training and 350,000 evaluation daily records; hourly stockout/sales information; CC BY 4.0 | Strong new source for a separately defined stockout experiment with RF/DT. It is not the existing backorder target and lacks verified IILAS reservations, replenishment history and three-class labels. Do not concatenate it with Backorder or feed its outcomes into current inference. |
| 3 — Delivery | [Olist original publisher and order dictionary](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce?select=olist_orders_dataset.csv): approximately 100,000 orders from 2016–2018; the order viewer reports 99,441 unique order IDs; CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 | Strong candidate for a separate binary lateness benchmark. The dictionary explicitly identifies actual customer delivery date and the estimated date supplied at purchase. File integrity, date semantics, missing outcomes, joins and eligibility still require local audit. Download authentication was not tested. |
| Complementary — Parcel | [Parcel2D Real](https://zenodo.org/records/8031971): approximately 400 real cuboid-parcel images, 2.3 GB archive, CC BY 4.0 | Useful intact-parcel domain/false-positive checks after annotation and grouping. The associated [Parcel3D paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2304.08994v1) states its real evaluation covers intact parcels. This is not a complete real Normal/Damaged test set and cannot establish damage recall or two-class precision on its own. |
| Secondary — Inventory | [Retail Transactions and Stocks Data v1](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/27x8mjm8k4/1): publisher describes daily sales and on-hand stock for 40 stores and 2,326 SKUs; inventory 95.4 MB, sales 37.3 MB; CC BY 4.0 | Potential source of relevant stock observations. Exact schema/keys, row counts, measurement timing, business provenance, reservations and replenishment outcomes remain unverified. The publisher's real-business description is not an independent provenance audit. |

FreshRetailNet needs special semantic checks. Its card lists 865 SKUs while the [original paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.16319v1) states 863; count the pinned release. The card describes hourly out-of-stock status, whereas the paper's mathematical mask uses one for availability: verify the file/code encoding before defining a target. Sales are scaled, and true lost demand during genuine stockouts is unobserved. Do not turn reconstructed demand into independently observed ground truth or use same-period stockout outcomes as predictors of that outcome.

Olist's `order_delivered_customer_date` is an outcome; `order_estimated_delivery_date` is described as known at purchase. Define the prediction cutoff and date-only deadline interpretation before modelling. Do not automatically treat midnight estimates as exact early-morning deadlines, missing delivery dates as failures, or cancellations as High risk. Final status, completion timestamps and later customer reviews cannot be predictors for an earlier decision. Repeated items/orders and customer/product relationships require explicit grouping and temporal checks. Olist can support an independent-source experiment, but is not automatically an external test for DataCo's five-feature model.

## Concrete next steps within the existing core scope

1. **Expand parcel metadata first.** Inspect the original release index/configuration metadata and count distinct base roots and related families. Select a deterministic, versioned expansion favouring new families and both source conditions. Set a byte budget and choose only required image/config/annotation members where supported. Verify every acquired member and preserve all old files. No score-driven image selection.
2. **Review expanded labels and dependencies before final splits.** Keep the existing six quarantined images quarantined. Preserve uncertain/excluded states; review retained candidates rather than accepting publisher flags automatically. Keep all related views, deformations and augmentations within a group. Do not call repeated renders independent evidence. Select training/validation/test once; use training-only augmentation. Evaluate real and synthetic domains separately.
3. **Audit FreshRetailNet and Olist in separate ignored collections.** Pin release, licence and hashes; inspect a minimised schema and aggregate quality summary, not raw identifiers/reviews in logs. Verify target encoding, observation times, missing outcomes, independent units and duplicate relationships. Define eligibility and the new task before fitting. Retain benchmark-native labels; no invented primary three-class targets.
4. **Continue the existing tabular improvement plan unchanged.** Existing data are sufficient to execute its bounded development experiments; the source review does not establish statistical sufficiency for final deployment. Use OOF/fixed-validation predictions, not the already-exposed test set, to compare precision/recall tradeoffs. A new-source experiment needs its own preregistration and untouched confirmation partition; never concatenate incompatible schemas merely to increase rows.
5. **Judge measured improvement.** Report precision, recall, macro F1, AP, confusion/alert counts and group-aware uncertainty. Higher precision accompanied by collapsed recall is a tradeoff, not an unqualified improvement. There is no promised accuracy percentage or universally sufficient image count.

The primary IILAS Normal/Reorder Risk/Critical Stock Risk and Low/Medium/High delivery-risk evaluations remain unfulfilled by these public binary targets. No reviewed source closes all feature/outcome gaps. This review is research evidence and a source shortlist, not an acquisition log or new model result. Raw datasets, images, row-level decisions and models must remain outside GitHub.
