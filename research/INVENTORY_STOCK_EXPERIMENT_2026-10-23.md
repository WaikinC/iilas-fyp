# Simulated stock classifier: first bounded experiment

Date: 23 October 2026. User-approved AI-assisted simulated prototype, with independent accuracy validation pending. This is an offline research checkpoint; the application's stock rules/review workflow remains unchanged.

## Data and selection

The [training protocol](INVENTORY_TRAINING_PROTOCOL_2026-10-23.md) and [AI-assisted method](INVENTORY_AI_ASSISTED_PROTOCOL_2026-10-23.md) were fixed before fitting. Main-01 contains300 fictional product families, one primary observation per family:240 development and60 held-out,20 per class. Its manifest SHA-256 remains `8ef1f9a86466e63b54349a8cd34c6a62ca50fc4f9f6354be9d317bbd43da3962`. All60 original pilot families are excluded. No private business records or Kaggle/public sales rows were used.

Labels are generated by the disclosed AI-authored deterministic context policy, not independently observed warehouse outcomes. The six predictors are available quantity, reserved quantity, movement frequency, reorder distance, adjustment count and days since replenishment. Commitment deadlines and supply context can affect the teaching label but are absent from these predictors. Constructed class balance, correlated policy inputs and this missing context limit interpretation.

Eight predeclared settings received three grouped development folds:24 fits. Selection used mean macro-F1, then Critical recall, smaller depth and larger leaf. Selected settings were DT depth4/leaf2 and RF100 trees/depth6/leaf2; both balanced class weights/seed42, RF n_jobs2. Each received one full-development fit, giving26 total. Both choices and model bytes were frozen before held-out observations were loaded. Their joint prediction arrays and class order were saved and hashed before any test metric. No test-guided refit, replacement seed or model-family promotion occurred.
