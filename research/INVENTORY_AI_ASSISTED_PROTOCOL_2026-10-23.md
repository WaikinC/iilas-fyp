## Annotation and interpretation

The 60 assistant draft assessments supply the transparent pilot rubric, not a final evaluation set. Main examples are new fictional families and exclude all pilot families/ancestry. An AI-authored deterministic labelling policy operationalises the pilot reasoning from cutoff-known commitments and supply context. It is a synthetic teacher, not a separate AI vision/language model, independent human review or observed stockout truth. Do not describe its automated application as a human inspecting every record. Metadata must identify this method as `AI-authored synthetic policy` and keep `independent_human_review: false` and `operationally_approved: false`.

Critical Stock Risk requires additional firm demand beyond already available units, with stated disrupted, too-late or confirmed-but-insufficient supply that cannot cover the deadline without corrective action. When confirmed supply is timely but insufficient, quantify the remaining shortfall after counting that confirmed supply once. Reorder Risk covers an at/below-policy buffer or a timely but unconfirmed supply plan needing confirmation. Normal requires adequate stock and documented commitments/supply context. Existing reservations are counted once. Inconsistent/missing context is rejected, not relabelled Normal. These are explicit synthetic assumptions; unconfirmed supply can mean different real risks depending on reliability that the simulation does not measure.

Performance on these labels measures agreement with this synthetic teacher under the constructed scenario distribution. It cannot prove independent inventory-risk accuracy or justify an 80% real-application claim. Class balance and policy-correlated features can inflate apparent performance. Keep the six-feature model's missing commitment/supply context visible and report failures without tuning against held-out outcomes.

## Main data and coverage

Prepare exactly 300 new independent fictional product families with one primary cutoff each, using base generator seeds1001-1005. Use prospective strata of100 acceptable-operation contexts,100 replenishment-review contexts and100 likely-impact contexts. Coherent labels are separately calculated from replayed balances and context, not copied from a class name or ID; the method remains policy-generated rather than independent truth. Validate all cases and refuse inconsistent support instead of silently replacing cases. Do not change seeds or label rules after test exposure to improve scores.

The explicit strata express the student prototype design, not real class prevalence. Normal strata have recorded free stock above their policy threshold and no additional unsupported commitment. Reorder strata require replenishment review, with a timely but unconfirmed supply plan when demand exceeds free units. Critical strata have a specific uncovered additional commitment and disrupted supply after its deadline. Quantities, reservations and activity vary; current stock magnitude alone is not the Critical definition. Reorder levels and context are synthetic design variables, not imported real inventory fields.

Cycle six predeclared coverage conditions across the300 cases: actual receipt at cutoff, receipt a quarter-day earlier, no recent receipt with the latest eligible receipt older than30days, complete zero-event30day window, latest receipt90days earlier, and ordinary history. To remove recent receipts while retaining coherent balances, compensate the fictional opening quantity by their total and replay every remaining event. For a zero-event window retain complete older history and recompute all current quantities/commitments. If positive free stock is required for an acceptable-operation scenario, add a fictional opening unit and replay; do not clamp a bad balance or fabricate a real receipt. Changes are new main trajectories, never edits to pilot-01.

All current stock facts, event/correction lineage, coverage, receipt eligibility and six features use the existing pure assessment contract. Main labels may use documented context absent from the six model inputs; this limitation is not repaired by secretly adding those fields to a predictor. Each case binds its new base family, source seed, coverage condition, feature/rubric/teacher versions and actual evidence hashes. No private/production data, public sales dataset or old training cohort is used.
