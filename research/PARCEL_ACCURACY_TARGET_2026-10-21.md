## What the investigation establishes

1. **Longer training alone is unsupported.** All four original histories have their best validation loss in epoch1or2. Training loss subsequently falls while validation loss worsens. In retained MobileNetV2, training loss falls0.8413→0.5850, while validation reaches its best0.7657in epoch2and ends0.7980. This is an overfitting/poor-transfer pattern, not proof of one unique cause. Training uses augmentation/dropout, so absolute TRAIN/VAL loss gaps are not same-input accuracy gaps. No per-epoch training accuracy was logged.
2. **Real fine-tuning was tried.** The conservative MobileNetV2 tail candidate trained1,513,282parameters with low learning rate and frozen BatchNormalization. On the same133validation images, accuracy fell56.39%→53.38% and component macroF1 fell0.564825→0.557018. Damage recall rose39.62%→54.72%, but false alarms rose26→38. It remains rejected under the original balanced selection rule. Repeating that recipe is not a new improvement experiment.
3. **No current normalization/checkpoint bug was established.** Existing pretrained compatibility receipts support correct learned tensors, input scaling, synthetic canonical-feature and save/load parity. Source checks agree on RGB, class order, softmax/loss, optional CLAHE,224px full-frame letterboxing and restored best checkpoints. The diagnosis did not reopen saved classifiers for a new actual-pixel replay; it must not claim a new complete runtime parity test.
4. **Visible supervision needs review.** A deterministic, prediction-blind six-image TRAIN-only probe (three per class, distinct conservative groups) found printed retail-carton appearances and ambiguous visible condition. Two of three publisher-Damaged views had no clear physical defect identified by the assistant; a third had an ambiguous flap/end feature. This tiny convenience sample neither estimates label error nor proves publisher labels wrong. Printed packaging alone does not establish that a package is outside the parcel task. Source item identity, single-view visibility and actual shipping use remain separate questions.
5. **The architectures share the main constraints.** Both receive the same small cohort, source labels, views,224px representation and validation boundary. A new backbone might help, but no evidence yet establishes architecture as the main bottleneck. Neither contrast enhancement nor another network supplies an unseen face or resolves an unobservable label.

## Recommended order

1. **Audit public TRAIN suitability first.** Predeclare a larger prediction-blind, group-separated relevance/visibility review using the existing report's visible-condition rubric. Keep publisher labels immutable and record separate tentative visibility/condition observations, uncertainties and disagreements. Do not select or discard cases using model errors. Qualify source rights and whether labels describe the shown face before calling a new cohort IILAS-relevant.
2. **Expand only qualified public development data.** Use the existing verified archive where suitable, or a better licensed public source. Preserve exact provenance, all related views/groups and technical failures. Keep every known development/review/test exposure documented. More volume is not itself better supervision, and a carton material flag is not enough.
3. **Test one primary-model improvement after that audit.** Retain MobileNetV2 as the report's primary model and EfficientNetB0 as comparator. A separately declared expanded-data frozen-backbone baseline can distinguish a data gain from a later fine-tuning gain. Choose any subsequent low-rate partial unfreezing, augmentation or resolution change from TRAIN/VAL evidence and an actual resource feasibility check; do not bundle an unconstrained search or simply increase epochs. Preserve BatchNormalization state and existing original artifacts. Keras describes fine-tuning as optional, with very low learning rates and careful BatchNormalization handling: [official guide](https://keras.io/guides/transfer_learning/).
4. **Evaluate the final fixed choice once on fresh relevant groups.** Define80%as a future held-out image-accuracy objective with complete denominators, both classes, damage precision/recall, macroF1, coverage and group-aware uncertainty. Preserve balanced macroF1-based development selection unless prospectively changed; do not meet80%by a majority-class shortcut. Freeze labels, exclusions, model, preprocessing and decisions before the new evaluation. The completed122-image internal test is consumed and cannot be reused as untouched evidence; avoiding test-guided choices follows [the official leakage guidance](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/common_pitfalls.html).
5. **Consider another architecture only if the evidence supports it.** A new model is a bounded future comparator experiment, not a replacement for data/target work or a guarantee of80%. The existing report stack remains authoritative. No additional architecture was installed or trained here.

Reaching80%on a publisher benchmark would still not establish80%IILAS photo/live accuracy. Relevant independent parcel evidence, visible labels and physical grouping, justified acceptance criteria, and actual camera latency/drop/resource measurements remain required. Public data cannot measure the intended physical camera/browser/network configuration. Both real inference gates remain closed. No production, deployment, publication or GitHub data/model upload occurs.
