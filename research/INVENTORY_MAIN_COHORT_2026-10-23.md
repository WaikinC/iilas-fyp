## Frozen data

The new main-01 cohort is separate from all 60 pilot families: 300 fictional product families, one primary observation each, 307 files and 2,123,419 bytes. It has 100 synthetic-policy examples per report class: Normal, Reorder Risk and Critical Stock Risk. Its fixed partition contains 240 development families and 60 reserved test families, with 20 test families per class. This balanced design is not an estimate of real risk prevalence.

The labels are generated by an AI-authored, explicit context-aware teaching policy, not independent human annotation or observed warehouse outcomes. A future score can measure model agreement with that policy on its controlled scenarios. It cannot demonstrate independent real inventory accuracy. The six predictors omit commitment/supply context used by the policy; learning that distinction is not guaranteed.

Fifty cases each cover a same-day receipt, quarter-day receipt, latest receipt older than 30 days, complete zero-activity window, 90-day-old receipt and ordinary history. These conditions are distributed across all classes. Balances, reservations, receipt/correction lineage, complete fictional history and six features are replay-validated, with pilot ancestry excluded. The zero-activity window has actual complete older history; it does not reinterpret a missing real ledger as zero.

Every case has a separate provenance entry binding its family/ancestry, base seed, coverage condition, feature/rubric/teacher versions and evidence hash. These fields are not predictor columns. Source/file hashes and deterministic regeneration bind the packet; a successful audit does not make the fictional examples statistically independent of their shared generation program.
