## Automated database tests

`scripts/smoke-firestore.mjs` passed product create/read/update/archive, image/category persistence, duplicate-key rejection, shipment linkage, stock deduction, insufficient-stock rejection and account isolation.

`scripts/qa-review.mjs` passed simultaneous dispatch competition, stale-edit rejection, direct invalid-write rejection, preservation of archived references, protection of current SKU reservations and rejection of dispatch without matching stock deduction. Both two-product creation directly as Dispatched and updating Prepared to Dispatched passed; preparation left stock unchanged and dispatch reduced both products together.

All security probes ran against local Firebase emulators. Expected permission-denied messages were assertions, not unexpected failures. One emulator restart needed cleanup of a leftover local Java process; the completed test runs exited with code 0.

## Remaining improvements

All three usability findings were implemented and deployed on 15 September 2026:

1. Failed saves scroll the modal error into view and move keyboard focus to it. A live insufficient-stock check confirmed the message was visible at the top of the narrow-screen dialog.
2. The “At or below reorder level” dashboard card now opens a labelled low-stock view showing only the two matching products, with a **Show all products** action.
3. The shipment picker now displays the count as “Selected items (x of 2)” and shows an explicit limit-reached message after the second selection.

The remaining production-scale limitations are the two-product Firestore transaction cap, catalogue/activity pagination, and full dialog focus trapping.
