## Review Focus

1. Finalisation writes succeed partly or are retried after acknowledgement loss: experiment, receipt, one alert/requirement and audit must commit together, with no duplicates or old-result backfill (Task2).
2. Product changes after prediction, or after a completed review: new stale Accept must fail, stale Reject must remain possible, and matching terminal recovery must retain its original decision without current product/model configuration (Task3).
3. A review dialog closes, account changes or another saved review replaces intent during a late reply: no stale UI update/key deletion; all existing inference keys remain untouched (Task4).
4. Workflow capability/profile/source pin changes while model HTTP is running: no risk item or prediction may escape configuration fencing; old profiles must explicitly disable inherited capability (Tasks2/5).
5. A forged alert/requirement refers to another experiment or changes scores/source facts while keeping plausible IDs: detail/review must reject mismatched reciprocal lineage, with no independent-label or operational promotion (Tasks1/3).
