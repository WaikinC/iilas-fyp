## Staff workflow

1. Use a selected demonstration product in Stock Management's Stock AI experiment, entering the eight fictional context facts explicitly. A supported new risk result retains a separate demo alert and reorder requirement.
2. A different Admin or Manager reviews the requirement in Replenishment & Procurement. Acceptance records a demo workflow decision; it does not approve the model, purchase goods or change stock.
3. Open the accepted requirement and select **Prepare or view demo purchase**. Its original evidence and exact Accept event are checked. A changed product needs a fresh analysis/review before creating its first order. An existing permanent link opens its order history instead.
4. Enter your own purchase reference, active supplier, whole-unit quantity, MYR unit cost and expected date. The single linked product is fixed. No quantity or supplier is inferred from AI scores or fictional demand.
5. A different Admin or Manager approves the purchase order. The requirement reviewer may approve the PO if they are not its requester. Rejected/Submitted orders can be corrected and resubmitted for that same product, resetting purchase approval.
6. Explicitly issue an approved order. Only this step creates its expected-incoming record; stock stays unchanged. The prototype records/simulates supplier issue and sends no supplier message.
7. Record actual demonstration deliveries with their own delivery-note references. Partial and full receipts atomically update purchase quantities, expected incoming, location-aware stock and movement history. Received incoming remains historical with zero outstanding units.

Each accepted requirement has one permanent PO link. Cancelling an unissued order does not release it; a replacement requires a new analysis and independent review. Historical/manual orders are not backfilled or silently attributed to an AI requirement. Expected dates and overdue displays do not prove physical receipt or supplier confirmation.
